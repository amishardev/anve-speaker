What the light on the box means

  • Two short blinks

    Online. Open the link to add it to your account.

  • Three short blinks

    On your Wi-Fi, but the network is blocking ANVE. The box keeps trying. If it stays like this, choose another network, or ask whoever manages this one to allow ANVE.

  • Slow pulse

    It couldn't connect. Join the setup Wi-Fi again to see why.
